# Biography


I am a postdoctoral researcher in Applied Artificial Intelligent (AAI) lab at Penn State University. My research focuses on applying formal methods to transportation cyber-phsyical systems (CPS). I focus on designing ways to supervise and assure CPS controlled by machine-learning (ML)and artificial-intelligence (AI) systems, so that the black-box nature of these systems can be made safety-critical. 

I received my PhD from the Vanderbilt University Civil Engineering department, where I worked on a variety of topics in traffic control and vehicle automation. In 2020 I was awarded an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship to study high reliance traffic control with automated vehicles.
